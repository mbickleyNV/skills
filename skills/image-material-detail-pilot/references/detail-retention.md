# Recover detail lost between a hypothesis and native material

Use when an approved appearance study is rich but the native material is smooth,
uniform or weak. Keep original-photo fidelity and matching an approved generated
hypothesis as separate targets. If the user selects the hypothesis as the new
visual target, record that choice; do not silently revert to a conservative photo
interpretation or claim the invented detail was measured from the photograph.

## Find the first actual loss

Make a short feature ledger for the selected target: intermediate shapes (bundle,
flake cluster, tool mark), finer structure within them, broad finish regions,
independent outer-interface response, and quiet regions. Record each feature's
width/count relative to the specimen and approximate pixels at delivery size.
A generic adjective such as “fine” is not a scale contract. Broad finish islands, intermediate structure and fine relief may need independent coordinate scales; a single tiling value can shrink every population together even when every map is connected. Increasing repeats to
make a coarse generated tile look small can erase the intermediate shapes that
made the study convincing. More normal amplitude cannot recover an absent scale.

For each defining feature, inspect this chain:

`approved target crop → generated field → converted field → complete graph and
composed values → evaluated field on geometry → native reflection crop → beauty`.

Read the graph, not only textures. Follow inherited defaults, image fallbacks,
color transforms, powers, remaps, masks, normal conversion, layer mixture and
outer-interface inputs to their actual destinations. A scalar beside a connected
socket may be only its missing-texture fallback. A material name or metadata label
does not establish an active coat. Count meaningful independently controlled
responses rather than nodes, texture files or nonzero lobes.

## Inspect effective ranges and slope populations

For scalar fields, report occupied-domain percentiles before and after all remaps,
not just nominal endpoints. A map near mid-gray multiplied by a narrow range can
be almost constant despite a visibly varied preview. Label every statistic as full-tile, occupied-on-mesh, analytic estimate, or native-evaluated; these are different evidence levels. Compare relevant regional
means/spreads and feature-correlated differences. Avoid global histogram stretching
as an automatic fix: choose a physically plausible proposed response range, retain
its uncertainty, and test the resulting reflection spread. Treat sparse masks by
coverage and nonzero-region statistics; whole-image percentiles can miss them.

For normals, inspect angular deviation and direction distributions after decoding,
strength, basis transfer and composition. Record units and physical slope when
using height: height amplitude / lateral distance matters; a purple image and a
strength value of 1 say little by themselves. Separate broad substrate shape,
resolved fine relief and unresolved roughness. A high percentile near grazing may
produce facets, black wedges or unstable glints rather than useful tactile detail.
Bracket strength while holding layout and roughness fixed; judge feature profile,
not just the size of an image delta.

Check the actual generated resolution and mip/filter footprint. Compare raw and
denoised crops at adequate samples only when denoising is a plausible cause; do
not blame denoising before verifying the field, scale and graph. Preserve the
standard lighting/camera for the main comparison. A separate grazing-light probe
can diagnose relief but cannot substitute for detail visible in the delivery view.

## Test construction, not an indiscriminate bump increase

Assign each observed response to its interface/population. Useful alternatives
include a shaped substrate with fine relief, spatially different conductor finish,
a conformal coating with independent microstructure, a smoother outer coating over
rougher substrate, or separate crossing strand populations. Add only alternatives
that explain a missing visible feature; extra lobes do not guarantee complexity.

A true coat has its own weight/coverage, IOR, roughness and normal relationship to
the base. Record shared coarse shape, smoothed components and independent residual
relief. Copying all base normals into the coat can duplicate a response; using a
perfectly smooth coat can bury it under broad highlights. Test combined, coat-off,
base-relief-off and coat-relief-off where those alternatives are consequential.
Layer-removal deltas include throughput changes and are not additive coat images.

For metal, distinguish conductor F0 color, grazing tint, overall reflection weight,
roughness and coverage by another material. Consult the installed model: OpenPBR
1.1.1 defines specular weight as an overall metallic Fresnel multiplier as well as
a dielectric reflectivity control. Do not declare it inert on metals by analogy to
another shader. A mixed dirt response is not a transparent physical dirt layer;
read actual closure composition before naming it.

Route visible gaps, over-under occlusion, parallax or silhouette depth to geometry
or a supported displacement construction when mapped normals cannot provide them.
Keep that geometry separate from the user's fixed benchmark. Do not promise that
a normal-only reconstruction reproduces every closeup property of raised strands.

## Compare and carry forward

Use the same selected reflection family and display for original/control variants.
Judge target-matched feature density, width spread, connectivity, highlight breakup
and coexistence of sharp glints with broad response. A small noisy difference does
not establish mapped influence; a large difference does not establish fidelity.
Keep the strongest prior candidate when a new layer masks its defining detail.
Expanded finish contrast can create coarse blotches that are absent from the target;
inspect the normal-off control to separate those blotches from actual relief.
Separate coordinate scales only for independent populations: a defect mask and its
associated relief must remain registered. Preserve quiet regions rather than
maximizing visible variation everywhere.

Record the selected construction and remaining failed criteria. Deliver a matched
old/new/target sheet with a resolved crop and the decisive control. Preserve the
original benchmark geometry, explicit coordinate repairs, input/map revisions and
all effective ranges. Feed reusable mechanism findings into the existing routing
skills, not a new recipe for each material name.

Primary model reference: [OpenPBR 1.1.1](https://academysoftwarefoundation.github.io/OpenPBR/).
For editable graph examples and their limitations, read
[exemplar construction notes](../../image-material-architecture/references/built-up-exemplars.md).


If generated height repeatedly contains clear directional illumination, reject that field rather than relabeling its grayscale as elevation. A useful alternative is an explicitly unlit feature-location mask plus declared analytic cross-sections. Separate the generated locations from the synthesized ridge/valley profiles and microstructure, record dimensions, and inspect the native result. This is a designed geometry hypothesis, not depth recovered from the photograph. Confirm that sparse linework has not become cracks, engraved veins or an overly regular fold pattern.
