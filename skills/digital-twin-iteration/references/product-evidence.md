# Product evidence before installation

Identify exact model, size, color/finish, shade, handedness and mounting variant.
Prefer manufacturer dimension drawings, assembly insets and finish close-ups.
Record source URL/page and distinguish measured, manufacturer-specified, inferred
and user-confirmed facts. A retailer image or generated concept is useful visual
intent; it is not dimensional or radiometric evidence.

Compare envelope and mounting datums against the actual receiving source before
selecting a size. Keep clearance, door motion, service and connector space separate
from visual fit. Resolve requested CAD-only scope before preparing a Blender import.

For a detail pass, inspect hidden-facing cutouts, lip/neck profiles, supports,
diffusers, seams and attachment insets that affect the intended view. Avoid a
generic silhouette standing in for reference-specific construction. Use the named
mesh drawing overlay in `luacad-product-reconstruction` when dimensioned drawings
exist. Revisit affected pages for revisions rather than rereading the entire manual.

For lights, record CCT, output, distribution and optic separately. Published lumens
are not automatically a Blender energy value. Label guessed bulb output, approximate
beam shape and empirical renderer calibration. Mount emitters at the actual optic;
verify contribution with a light-on/off comparison, holding exposure fixed for a
quantitative comparison. Do not claim photometric accuracy from matching color.
