## Finish-only image-generation concepts

Use the current verified render/contact sheet as the sole geometry and framing
reference. Label other images as color/material/detail inspiration only. Preserve
all dimensions, cabinet seams/counts, appliance platforms, sink/counter outlines,
openings, door swings and stair elevations. Keep one coherent treatment across
views; never import a reference room's layout or turn a solid door into glazing.
Inspect the generated result against the source; correct architectural drift.
Keep concepts/prompts in scratch until accepted, separate from canonical render
JPGs. Image-generation pixels are not dimensional evidence or permission to
change CAD. Translate accepted finishes/details only in a subsequent scoped pass.
