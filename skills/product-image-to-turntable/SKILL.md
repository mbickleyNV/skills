# Product image to CAD, materials and turntable

Coordinate the existing geometry and material workflows around one product record.
Preserve the user's reference and requested output. A successful mesh, an attractive
generated study and a completed movie establish different things.

## Establish the working contract

Inspect the actual supplied image. Record visible silhouette, proportions, openings,
edge profiles, surface regions and defining finish features, with source crops. A
single photograph cannot establish hidden construction, precise dimensions or a
unique shader. Use labeled, editable assumptions; ask for a dimension anchor only
when it changes a consequential decision. If single-image reconstruction is being
tested, keep product research and additional views out of the evidence set unless
the user authorizes them. General material research may inform hypotheses.

Read the active project's instructions and locate its maintained LuaCAD exporter,
Blender preparation path and native renderer/client. These skills do not install
them. Verify actual interfaces before execution; example helper paths in sibling
skills are external contracts. Use the project's official supported runtime. If an
adapter is absent, complete supported stages and identify the missing stage without
inventing commands, substituting a renderer or claiming a movie exists. A requested
turntable authorizes preparing a private studio when necessary; reuse an accepted
project studio when one exists. Do not change shared studios or live scenes silently.

Before dispatch, identify the actual exporter/version, preparation consumer, material
graph interface, native capture client and studio. Record each as verified, to be
qualified or unavailable. Historical house/scratch paths, resource limits and local
worker transports in sibling examples do not override the active project's tools,
artifact layout or runtime boundary.

Create a compact [geometry/material handoff](references/geometry-material-handoff.md)
in the project's task-owned output area. It links source, dimension assumptions,
stable part/surface IDs, geometry units, material cases and delivery state. Reuse
accepted evidence when its inputs and consumer remain unchanged.

## Route and resolve the coupled decisions

1. **Geometry:** use [LuaCAD fidelity modeling](../luacad-fidelity-modeling/SKILL.md),
   which selects the four focused reconstruction/construction skills. Keep editable
   CAD source and stable named exports. Match silhouette and section before fine
   tessellation. Build actual walls, cavities, bevels, seams and gaps when they affect
   silhouette, contact, reflection or optical path length. Do not invent hidden
   mechanisms. Validate dimensions/topology and inspect the exported geometry.
2. **Material:** use [image-material-analyze](../image-material-analyze/SKILL.md)
   for each distinct surface family, reusing equivalent cases across parts. Carry
   source anchors through architecture, generated appearance studies, coordinated
   maps, native assembly and review. Use its conditional spatial/detail/diagnostics
   routes where evidence calls for them. Keep geometry work independent until a
   coupled decision such as wall thickness, relief or fiber silhouette is resolved.
3. **Interface:** use the handoff reference to assign geometry versus displacement,
   base/coat normal ownership, UV or projection frames, physical feature size and
   transport distances. Resolve these before final maps or asset preparation. Never
   infer roughness from bright edges alone, bake source lighting into albedo, or
   use stronger normal maps to conceal missing product shape.
4. **Consumer:** use [product asset preparation](../product-asset-preparation/SKILL.md)
   for a Blender delivery. Preserve exact dimensions, region bindings, authored
   render normals, per-corner UVs, map encodings and the intended graph. Read back
   saved/reopened assignments. Only the live owner integrates and renders; delegate
   disjoint work only when authorized. A materially changed consumer/translator
   needs a bounded native check even if the source graph already rendered elsewhere.
   A preparation packet limited to base color/roughness/normal or a few Principled
   inputs cannot represent an arbitrary layered MaterialX graph. Qualify a supported
   graph-preserving consumer; do not silently reduce the material to those fields.
   Any necessary approximation remains explicit and appearance-critical losses stay
   unresolved until reviewed on the actual native consumer.
5. **Image acceptance:** inspect a source-matched product view plus a detail view
   where needed. Separate silhouette/geometry, feature morphology, color/exposure,
   layer response and mapping issues. For a new complex material, retain the same
   accepted standard UV shader ball and diagnostic lighting across trials; do not
   substitute a custom ball. Use product geometry for shape/thickness and source
   comparison. The ball cannot prove product transport, and a flattering product
   view cannot prove material mechanisms. Reuse unchanged accepted ball evidence.
6. **Movie:** use [product-turntable](../product-turntable/SKILL.md) after the saved
   asset is accepted. Preserve its receipt, ownership and native capture gates.
   A maintained native-USD project route may instead consume a frozen dependency-
   complete stage with equivalent reviewed geometry/material/input hashes and
   actual native bindings. Label this route explicitly; it does not satisfy or
   bypass a Blender asset receipt. Do not implement a second bridge for it.

Use mechanism-selected AOVs/LPEs and matched controls from
[material diagnostics](../image-material-diagnostics/SKILL.md) for disputed optical
responses. Inspect raw finite buffers and native parameter influence, not only
maps or a generic diffuse/glossy pair. For scratched/coated surfaces, test the
substrate detail and outer interface separately; use evidence to choose whether
fine scratches are buried, exposed or both. For textiles, distinguish folds, yarn
topology and fiber response instead of adding branching ridge noise.

## Finish the requested deliverable

Before the full orbit, inspect distinct quarter views for framing, ground contact,
back/underside assumptions, binding, texture scale and directional response. When
mapping or fine detail is uncertain, also inspect consecutive rotation frames;
four separated stills cannot expose popping, crawling or blend shimmer. Keep
lighting/exposure/display transform fixed during material comparisons. A white
background is a lighting setup, not a material color correction.

Correct the responsible source or material stage and recheck the affected view.
Do not compensate for a wrong normal basis with roughness, weak detail with baked
lighting, or incorrect coat routing by amplifying every normal. Triplanar removes
UV dependence; stochastic hex reduces repetition. Choose them per coordinate/feature
family, preserving long brushing, folds, bands and weave continuity. Neither is an
automatic upgrade for every map.

Deliver editable CAD, a named render asset/stage with dependencies, material cases
and actual graph/maps, one inspected representative image and the encoded native
turntable. Record output hashes, frame count/FPS and the chosen color pipeline in
the project receipt. Report **geometry fidelity**, **material fidelity**, **native
support** and **movie completion** separately, including meaningful unresolved
assumptions. Do not mark a source-identity failure accepted merely because the
movie completed. An interrupted job resumes only after reconciling current inputs
and existing outputs; do not mix source revisions or rerun an uncertain capture.

For CAD-only use fidelity modeling; for materials only start with material analysis;
for an accepted saved asset use product-turntable. Model completion alone does not
authorize unrelated installation, simulation, publication or turntable work.
